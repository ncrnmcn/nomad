# DELTA GREEN – UNIFIED SANITY DEGRADATION TABLE
*A Progressive Insanity System by Breaking Point*

---

## SANITY STRUCTURE

An Agent has **four Breaking Points (BP)**.  
Each Breaking Point increases **intensity, duration, and permanence** of mental damage.

| Breaking Point | State |
|---------------|------|
| BP1 | Fractured |
| BP2 | Compromised |
| BP3 | Unstable |
| BP4 | Burned Out |

When SAN loss triggers a Breaking Point:
- Roll **1d100** on the appropriate table
- Effects **stack** unless explicitly replaced
- Recovery requires therapy, time, or narrative intervention (GM’s mercy is not guaranteed)

---

## BP1 — FRACTURED  
*Cracks in the glass. Still functional. Mostly.*

**Duration:** 1d10 + 4 **Rounds or Minutes**

|   d100 | Effect                                                      |
| -----: | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
|  01–10 | Fainting or screaming fit                                   |
|  11–20 | Flees in panic                                              |
|  21–30 | Physical hysterics or emotional outburst (laughing, crying) |
|  31–40 | Incoherent babbling or logorrhea                            |
|  41–50 | Intense phobia; may freeze in place                         |
|  51–60 | Hallucinations or mild delusions                            |
|  61–70 | Echopraxia or echolalia                                     |
|  71–80 | Deviant eating urge (dirt, slime, blood)                    |
|  81–90 | Stupor (fetal position, oblivious)                          |
| 91–100 | Temporary paranoia                                          |

*Mechanical Guidance:*  
– Disadvantage on social tests  
– −20% to skill checks requiring focus

---

## BP2 — COMPROMISED  
*The Agent adapts… incorrectly.*

**Duration:** 1d10 × 10 **Hours**

| d100 | Effect |
|-----:|--------|
| 01–10 | Compulsive rituals (checking weapons, counting steps) |
| 11–20 | Persistent hallucinations or delusions |
| 21–30 | Moderate paranoia (trust checks required) |
| 31–40 | Severe phobia (DC 20 to approach trigger) |
| 41–50 | Aberrant sexual or fixation behaviors |
| 51–60 | Attachment to “lucky charm” or person |
| 61–70 | Psychosomatic blindness, deafness, or limb loss |
| 71–80 | Uncontrollable tics or tremors |
| 81–90 | Amnesia (recent memories, personal bonds) |
| 91–100 | Reactive psychosis episodes |

*Mechanical Guidance:*  
– −20% to all non-mental skill checks  
– Bonds erode faster; projecting SAN loss onto Bonds risks additional consequences

---

## BP3 — UNSTABLE  
*Identity degradation. Mission risk.*

**Duration:** 1d6 **Months**

| d100 | Effect |
|-----:|--------|
| 01–10 | Identity fracture (dissociation, altered behavior) |
| 11–20 | Severe paranoia (sees threat everywhere) |
| 21–30 | Hallucinations indistinguishable from reality |
| 31–40 | Compulsive violence (homicidal or suicidal ideation) |
| 41–50 | Fetishistic dependence (object/person required to act) |
| 51–60 | Loss of speech or written communication |
| 61–70 | Chronic psychosomatic disability |
| 71–80 | Catatonic episodes under stress |
| 81–90 | Emotional deadening or reckless disregard |
| 91–100 | Obsessive ideology, belief, or fixation |

*Mechanical Guidance:*  
– Bonds may permanently break  
– Agent behavior may override player intent during stress

---

## BP4 — BURNED OUT  
*Operationally dead. Sometimes biologically alive.*

**Duration:** Indefinite

| d100 | Outcome |
|-----:|---------|
| 01–20 | Permanent catatonia or institutionalization |
| 21–40 | Total identity collapse (NPC control) |
| 41–60 | Violent psychosis; Agent is a threat |
| 61–80 | Cold emotional void; incapable of Bonds |
| 81–100 | Delusional mission fixation (Agent pursues one false truth endlessly) |

*Mechanical Guidance:*  
– Agent is no longer viable  
– Retirement, containment, disappearance, or termination recommended

---

## OPTIONAL TRAITS (Assign at BP2+)

Choose or roll when appropriate:
- **Cold** – Emotionally detached
- **Haunted** – Persistent intrusive memories
- **Obsessed** – Singular fixation
- **Paranoid** – Constant threat perception
- **Reckless** – Disregard for safety
- **Soft** – Sentimental, passive
- **Unstable** – Volatile emotional shifts
- **Vicious** – Seeks harm opportunistically

Traits persist unless narratively resolved.

---

## FINAL NOTE

Sanity does not return to zero.  
It **rearranges itself**.

Delta Green does not break people.  
It *reveals what they were always going to become*.
