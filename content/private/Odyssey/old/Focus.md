# [FOCUS]
## _THE FOCUS TOKEN

### //OPEN FOCUS / CLOSED FOCUS

Players decide if a session is open or closed. Open sessions are represented by the FOCUS TOKE being placed on the table.

### //FOCUS IS OFFERED

At the start of play, each player declares whether they are open to sharing Focus. Only consenting players will be asked to describe scenes. A player may change this choice at any time.

The GM is the default story teller. He can shift the narrative focus to the consenting players by asking them to describe a scene.

Consent to Focus

After this shift the player can

- describe the scene without taking the FOCUS.

By doing this the player remains in character.

EXAMPLE: A player opens a safe. The GM asks the player what he finds inside. The player describes the contents of the safe without taking FOCUS of the whole scene.

- take the FOCUS and lead the story from there.

EXAMPLE: A player opens a safe. The GM asks the player what he finds inside and opens up the scene by lifting his  focus card.. Player can then take the FOCUS by paying 1FP. He describes the contents of the safe and describes how they group suddenly hears footsteps from the hallway, shortly before hooded minions storm the room.

The GM can add to the FOCUS of the player “And…”, “But…”.

Players cannot use FOCUS to manipulate other characters (Character B has a heart attack…).

The GM can retake the FOCUS by lifting his card. The Player can then extend the FOCUS by paying 1 FP or give the FOCUS back to the GM.

### //FOCUS IS REQUESTED

Player can also request the the FOCUS from the GM when it is not offered by paying 2 FP.

The GM can add to the FOCUS of the player “And…”, “But…”.

Players cannot use FOCUS to manipulate other characters (Character B has a heart attack…).

The GM can retake the FOCUS by lifting his card. The Player can then extend the FOCUS by paying 1 FP or give the FOCUS back to the GM.

### //LIMITS OF PLAYER FOCUS

Can:

- add twists to the story.
    
- alter the world 

Cannot:

- directly alter/harm/kill/revive NPCs/PCs.

### //IMPULSE & CODEWORDS

By raising the IMPULSE CARD a player can alter the scene. 

For the IMPULSE to have effect two other players have to confirm the IMPULSE by paying 1 FP each.

The GM can use impulses without any cost.

! IMPULSES have to articulate in an open way so they don‘t break immersion if the are not confirmed.
